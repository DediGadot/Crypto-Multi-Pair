SOS™ Framework (Sync Operating System)

The SOS™ architecture keeps concept, culture, and communication in sync through three interlocking layers. Every engagement touches each layer, but we enter through the one with the greatest leverage.

Layer 01

The Inner Self

Emotional clarity, regulation, embodied vision. Modalities include breathwork, somatic dialogue, and strategic reflection.

  • Outcome: grounded confidence
  • Format: 1:1 recalibration or immersive reset
Layer 02

The Organization

Relational diagnostics, culture mirrors, systemic leadership labs. We surface unseen patterns across the team.

  • Outcome: sync’d team energy
  • Format: founder circles, co-founder syncs
Layer 03

The Story

Authentic narratives for investors, customers, and talent. We translate the inner truth outward with pitch and PR coaching.

  • Outcome: magnetic storytelling
  • Format: story labs, investor narrative design
